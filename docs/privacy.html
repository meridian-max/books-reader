Books privacy policy

Effective 17 November 2026 · Books for Android · app.books.reader

Books is an ebook reader published by Divyansh007. Contact: developerg675@gmail.com.

Your library stays on your device

Books works without an account. Imported books, covers, reading positions, highlights, notes, bookmarks, reading history, statistics, settings and your optional profile name are stored in the app's local storage. Books has no advertising or analytics SDK and does not send your library or reading activity to a developer-operated server.

Files and device permissions

You choose individual files or folders through Android's system picker. Books accesses the locations you grant and can import, export or manage documents at your request. It does not request all-files access. Optional notifications and a media playback foreground service support read-aloud playback, including playback with the screen off. A wake lock can keep playback running. Optional fingerprint or device biometrics are handled by Android; Books receives the authentication result, never your biometric data. A locally configured app lock is stored as a salted password hash.

Optional cloud connections and exports

If you connect Dropbox or a WebDAV/Nextcloud server, Books sends reading positions, book identifiers, highlights, notes and reading sessions to the destination you selected when you use sync. If you upload a complete backup, it also contains your books, settings and local profile. Exports may include sensitive passages or notes. Connected-account credentials are excluded from backup files. Disconnecting stops future requests but does not delete copies already saved to your provider.

If you connect Readwise and choose to export, selected highlights, notes and book metadata are sent directly to Readwise. Its own terms and privacy policy apply. Connection credentials are encrypted in Android storage using Android Keystore. Books does not operate its own cloud account system. Google sign-in and Google Drive backup are not configured in version 1.9.1.

Downloads, catalogs and remote servers

When you browse an OPDS catalog, download a book, or connect an FTP/FTPS server, Books contacts that service directly. The service receives normal connection information such as your IP address and requested resource, plus credentials if you supplied them. Uploads and file operations occur only when you request them. Prefer HTTPS and FTPS: optional HTTP catalogs and plain FTP are not encrypted. A connected provider's privacy policy and retention rules apply.

Read aloud and optional voices

The optional Books neural voice pack is downloaded from the official sherpa-onnx project on GitHub only when you choose Download voices. The download host receives normal request information. The installed Books voice pack synthesizes book text locally on your device. If you select another Android text-to-speech engine, that engine receives the text to speak and may use its own online service. Its privacy policy applies; choose an offline voice to keep synthesis on your device.

Sharing and external lookups

Sharing passages, sending a word to a dictionary, opening a web search or translation service, and exporting files send the content you choose to the destination you select. Books does not control how those services process it.

Retention and deletion

You can remove books and their annotations from the library, edit or delete saved marks, disconnect cloud accounts, and clear the app's data in Android settings. Uninstalling or clearing app data removes local app records; externally exported files and remote backups remain until you delete them at their destination. Books does not have a developer-hosted account to delete. Requests about information you send to our support email can be made to that same address; support correspondence is retained only as needed to respond and maintain support records.

Security and changes

Cloud backup APIs use HTTPS, and saved connection credentials use Android Keystore encryption. Exported backup files are not password encrypted, so store and share them carefully. No storage or transmission method can be guaranteed completely secure. This policy will be updated when Books' data handling changes; the effective date above identifies this version.
